How do I book an appointment? +
Simply search for your desired procedure or clinic, select your preferred time slot, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation email immediately.

Yes! All clinics on DerMap are thoroughly vetted. We verify licenses, certifications, and credentials to ensure you receive safe, professional care.

Absolutely! You can cancel or reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before your scheduled time through your account dashboard.

Each procedure page includes detailed information about benefits, recovery time, and expected results. You can also consult directly with clinics through our platform.

Your privacy is our top priority. We use industry-standard encryption and never share your personal information with third parties without your consent.
